# Disclaimer

AI-CryptoFutures-TCP is engineering software for research, simulation, control-plane development, and future supervised trading workflows.

Nothing in this repository is financial advice, investment advice, or a recommendation to trade. Crypto futures are high-risk instruments. Losses can exceed expectations, especially when leverage, liquidation, latency, exchange outages, software defects, stale data, or operator error are involved.

Live trading is disabled by default and must not be enabled without explicit human approval, risk controls, tests, and operational review.
